# Mathematical statement and scope

The completed theorem is `KhanSL2.mainStatement : KhanSL2.MainStatement` in
[MainProof.lean](../lean/MainProof.lean). It has no remaining mathematical
hypotheses. The uppercase name denotes the proposition; the lowercase name
denotes its proof.

## The theorem in mathematical language

Work over the complex numbers. Let \(A\) be the differential supercommutative
algebra generated by an even element \(X\) and an odd element \(Y\), with all
their derivatives, subject to the differential ideal generated by

\[
X^2=XY=X\partial Y=Y\partial Y=0.
\]

Its generator brackets are

\[
\{X_\lambda X\}=\partial X+2\lambda X,\qquad
\{X_\lambda Y\}=\partial Y+3\lambda Y,\qquad
\{Y_\lambda Y\}=0.
\]

Let \(H\) be the **restricted, finite-mode relative CE cohomology** of
\(\mathfrak{sl}_2[x,y]/(y^2)\) relative to constant \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\), with
trivial complex coefficients, equipped with the specified induced product,
translation, and lambda bracket. Both polynomial variables \(x,y\) here are even.

Choose the color basis used in [ColorBasis.lean](../lean/KhanSL2/ColorBasis.lean).
Write \(b_j^a\) for the coordinate dual to \(t_a x^j y\), and \(c_{j+1}^a\)
for the coordinate dual to \(t_a x^{j+1}\). The specified cocycle classes are

\[
\mathsf{x}=\left[-\sum_{a=0}^{2}b_0^a\wedge c_1^a\right],\qquad
\mathsf{y}=[b_0^0\wedge b_0^1\wedge b_0^2].
\]

The explicitly constructed map

\[
\phi:A\longrightarrow H,\qquad
\partial^iX\longmapsto T^i\mathsf{x},\quad
\partial^iY\longmapsto T^i\mathsf{y}
\]

is a graded differential Poisson vertex superalgebra isomorphism. In particular,
it is bijective and preserves the unit, product, translation, every lambda-bracket
coefficient, and each homogeneous degree. Its definition descends evaluation
through the source relations; it is not chosen after proving the existence of
some isomorphism.

The source and target satisfy the full laws defined by KhanSL2.IsPVA:
an internal direct-sum grading, unital associative supercommutative product,
translation derivation, sesquilinearity, super skew symmetry, super Jacobi,
super Leibniz, and the stated bracket degree. These are proved independently
by source_isPVA and target_isPVA. The isomorphism predicate records the
preservation laws for these fixed structures.

## Relation to the manuscript

The paper's [Theorem 1.1](../paper/Pure_SU2_PVA_Proof.pdf) is the target
isomorphism statement. Equation (1.3) gives the source presentation; Section 2.2
explicitly uses finite polynomial cochains and graded duals; Section 2.3,
equation (2.10), gives the cocycle images above. The paper uses variables z,w
where this guide uses x,y. Colors are numbered 1,2,3 there
and 0,1,2 in Lean. The dictionary converts the paper's degree convention as
shown in the table.

There is a sign difference to record when reading the preserved manuscript:
its Section 2.2 commutator has a minus sign, while the checked
[q_translation_commutator](../lean/KhanSL2/CETranslation.lean) gives
\(Q T-T Q=+\sum_a c_1^a L_a\) for the formalized color-action convention.
Both expressions vanish on invariants; the formal proof uses its checked plus
sign. The formal proof uses the checked Strong Macdonald/Hodge development to
supply the factorization input corresponding to Section 6 and Appendix A.
The claim is the precise formal isomorphism theorem, not a line-by-line
formalization of every argument or consequence in the paper.

## What the formalization establishes, and what it does not assert

The notation \(H^*(\mathfrak{sl}_2[x,y]/(y^2),\mathfrak{sl}_2)\) in this release
always means the restricted finite-mode construction above. It must not silently
be read as the cohomology of an unrestricted algebraic dual or a completed
cochain space. A theorem identifying another convention with this one would be
a separate result.

The chain-side dictionary is proved from a general CE construction, including
the relative quotient and finite homogeneous duality. The project does not
claim an additional identification with an outside relative-cohomology library.
The source is a concrete generators-and-relations presentation; a separate
equivalence to mathlib's polynomial-algebra/exterior-algebra tensor product is
not part of the advertised theorem.

The accepted foundational axioms are propext, Classical.choice, and
Quot.sound. No additional mathematical axiom or unfinished proof is used by
the completed theorem. Formal proof checking establishes the exact Lean
proposition. Correspondence of its definitions to the intended mathematics is
documented here for review, rather than inferred merely from successful
compilation.


The Hilbert-series and constant-term consequences, physical interpretations, and
the coherent Satake identification in the PDF are not separately asserted as
formalized results by this repository's main-theorem entry point.
