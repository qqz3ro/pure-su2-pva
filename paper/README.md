# Paper

[Lie Algebra Cohomology and Seiberg-Witten Theory](Pure_SU2_PVA_Proof.pdf),
Ahsan Z. Khan, working draft dated October 25, 2026.

The supplied PDF is included unchanged. The main formalization target is
**Theorem 1.1**, with the differential algebra presentation in equation (1.3),
the finite polynomial cochain convention of Section 2.2, and the canonical
cocycles of equation (2.10) in Section 2.3.

The paper writes the current-algebra variables as `z,w`; the scope guide writes
them as `x,y`. In either notation the second variable is even and square-zero.

See [the scope guide](../docs/SCOPE.md) for normalization and sign conventions,
and [the declaration dictionary](../docs/DECLARATIONS.md) for the exact Lean
objects and theorems. The repository's main theorem formalizes the specified-map
PVA isomorphism; it does not by itself claim separate formalizations of every
physical interpretation, Hilbert-series consequence, or Satake-category
identification discussed in the paper.
